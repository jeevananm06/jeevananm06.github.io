Background & Philosophy

Bridging Low-Level Precision with Modern Scale

From first-principles engineering mechanics to high-concurrency enterprise retail architectures.

I am a Lead Software Engineer based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, surrounded by the foothills of the Western Ghats. My journey began with a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from Kumaraguru College of Technology (KCT), which instilled a rigorous foundation in mechanical computation, physical systems, and analytical problem-solving.

I transitioned that analytical rigor into software engineering at Cognizant, mastering low-level C and C++ programming on Unix/Linux systems—handling socket communication, multithreading, and memory-critical services.

For the past 8+ years at Lowe's Companies, Inc., I have applied this foundation to lead and architect mission-critical retail systems, high-volume Point of Sale (POS) applications, and microservices that power seamless customer transactions at massive scale.

Today, I specialize in building robust, decoupled, and high-performance backend systems using Python, FastAPI, C++, and cloud platforms. I am passionate about system design, code quality, developer velocity, and mentoring engineers to build resilient software.
